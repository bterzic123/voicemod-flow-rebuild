# Growth Audit — Voicemod

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/voicemod-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Voicemod already has, each rebuilt as a working screen in Voicemod's own design. Prepared by Adapty, based on the live iOS flow and the App Store listing.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Let them in before you ask for a login | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 02 | Ask what it is for, then build their soundboard | CR +10–15% |
| 03 | Put their voices and your 4.7 on the paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Ask for notifications after the paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | One second chance when they close | ARPU +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Voicemod. Every price, rating, quote, voice and feature line comes from Voicemod's own App Store listing, website and in-app screens.
